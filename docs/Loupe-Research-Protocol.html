2 · Research questions

Five questions, each with a hypothesis and a decision rule

RQ1 to RQ4 come from the HLD. RQ5 is new and proposed here. A question is only useful if we say in advance what result would answer it, so every row has a rule.

IdQuestionHypothesisDecision ruleRole
RQ1Transfer. Do FARE's conclusions hold when the architecture is rebuilt from the paper and run on SEC filings?Claims C1 to C7 in docs/fare/02-claims.md keep their direction on filings.Per claim: holds if the direction matches and the 95% CI excludes zero; partly if the direction matches and the CI includes zero; does not hold if the direction is reversed and the CI excludes zero. For rankings, "best model" holds if that model is ranked first in at least 80% of bootstrap resamples.Secondary
RQ2Multi-agent value. Do specialised agents beat one general agent given the same retrieval and the same model?B2 (FARE rebuild) scores higher than B1 (single agent, equal budget) on the pooled normalised score.Paired difference B2 − B1 on the test split, per model, company-cluster bootstrap. RQ3Judge reliability. RQ4Which stage matters. Which single stage change moves scores the most, and at what cost?Retrieval-side changes (reranker, hybrid search, section-aware chunks) move context precision more than any prompt or model change.One factor at a time from v0, on one model. RQ5Memorisation. Do scores reflect reading the filing, or what the model already knew?Without retrieval (B0), models answer numeric questions about filings dated before their knowledge cutoff noticeably better than about filings after it.B0 accuracy on Q-XBRL split by filing date relative to each model's published cutoff, with CI. If the gap exceeds the minimum detectable effect, every RQ1 and RQ2 result is also reported split by period.Proposed

Why RQ5 belongs here. FY2023 filings were public before Gemini 2.5's training cutoff; FY2025 10-Ks were filed in 2026, after every model in the set. That gives us a natural before/after split at no extra cost. Paper 8 in our folder (Yu, Liu and He, the "Ticker Identity Trap") shows that models can score well on earnings text by recognising the company rather than reading the text. A small anonymisation probe (50 queries with the company name and ticker replaced) tests the same effect on filings. FARE does not address memorisation at all.

3 · Company universe

19 companies, chosen by a written rule

The 50 files on Atharva's branch are CIKs 1750 to 5768 in strict ascending order, which means they are the first 50 entries of SEC's bulk companyfacts.zip rather than a hand-picked list. We keep them, but only companies that can support the experiments enter the universe.

INCLUSION RULE

A company is in the universe if its XBRL facts show, for fiscal years 2023 to 2025, at least 3 annual reports (10-K) and at least 6 quarterly reports (10-Q), filed under its own CIK as a US domestic filer.

WHAT THIS SAMPLE DOES WELL

Revenue spans $44M to $72B, three orders of magnitude. FARE used 21 mega-caps only. We can ask whether the agents work as well on small companies with thin, boilerplate filings, which FARE could not.

BIASES WE DECLARE

Low CIK numbers mean early EDGAR registrants, so these are long-established firms. Financials are heavy (AXP, AFL, AIG, UNM), and there is no software, retail or bank. Comparison queries have few same-sector pairs. We state this in the report and do not generalise beyond it.

SPLIT BY COMPANY, NOT BY QUERY

Prompts, batch size and every other setting are tuned only on the 6 dev companies. The 13 test companies are touched once, for the final runs. A query-level split would let prompts tuned on Abbott's 2024 10-K leak into test questions about Abbott's 2023 10-K.

Dev drawn at random within size bands (1 small, 2 mid, 3 large), seed 20261005. The team may re-draw once before the M0 freeze; after that the split is tagged in git and never changes.

PERIOD SPLIT FOR RQ5

Each filing is labelled before or after each model's published knowledge cutoff (from the model cards; record the source). FY2025 10-Ks, filed in 2026, are after every model in the set.

The harness refuses to run on test without an explicit flag and logs every test run, so the number of test runs can be reported.

Equal-budget rule for B1. If the single agent gets fewer chunks, fewer reading passes or fewer output tokens than the six agents, B2 wins for the wrong reason. 7 · Analysis plan

How numbers become claims

UNIT AND UNCERTAINTY

The unit is one query. Queries about the same company are not independent, so confidence intervals come from a company-cluster bootstrap: resample companies with replacement, 10,000 times, take the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles.

SCORES

Each agent's metric is rescaled to 0–1 as FARE defines it (TAEF ÷ 1, CAS as given, CODS (1–5) mapped to 0–1). The pooled score is the mean over agents, weighting each agent equally, so Comparison's 100 queries do not dominate.

MINIMUM DETECTABLE EFFECT

About 290 test queries, an assumed SD of 0.25 for paired differences, and a design effect of 2 for clustering give a minimum detectable effect of about 0.06 on the 0–1 scale (80% power, α = 0.05). Smaller differences will be reported as "not detectable with this sample", not as "no difference".

DETERMINISM

Temperature 0, a fixed thinking budget for Gemini 2.5 Flash, all prompts and configs hashed into every result row. Hosted models are still not fully deterministic, so 20% of B2 test queries are re-run once and the run-to-run difference is reported as a noise floor.

LATENCY AND COST

Benchmark runs may use batch mode, which halves price but makes timing meaningless. Latency claims (C2, C3) come from a separate 60-query standard-mode sample per model, run in the same hour.

WHAT GETS REPORTED

Every pre-registered comparison, whatever the result. Any analysis added after the freeze is labelled exploratory. Deviations from this protocol go in docs/fare/03-decisions.md with a date.

8 · Judge validation (RQ3)

Checking the judges against people before trusting them

FARE's own limitations section says its judge metrics were never validated by experts. This is the cheapest place for us to add something the paper lacks.

1

Write the rubric before grading

One rubric per agent, copied from FARE's metric definitions (NI, OT, NA for TAEF; FA and RA for CAS; and so on), with two worked examples each. Graders and judge prompts use the same text.

2

Two people grade H-120 blind

Graders see the query, the retrieved chunks and the answer, but not which model or system produced it, and not the judge's score. Inter-human weighted κ is the ceiling any judge can reasonably reach.

3

Compare each judge version with the human consensus

judge-v1 (FARE-style with Google Search), judge-v2 (frozen references), and on numeric items judge-v3 (objective XBRL checks). Report κ per agent with CIs.

4

Test for self-preference

judge-v1 runs on Gemini, which is also a generator. Compare the judge-minus-human gap on Gemini answers with the gap on Qwen and Llama answers. A systematic difference means Gemini's rankings in RQ1 need a second judge from another model family on the same items.
